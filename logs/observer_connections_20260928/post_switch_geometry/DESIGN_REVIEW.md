## Decision argument

The warmup advantage remains after both schedules use beta .9. Existing
validation cadence cannot decide whether its horizontal lead is constant or
eroding precisely. A retained advantage can reside in weights, representations,
radii, current gradients or adaptive state even when coefficients match.
"Momentum remembers the early phase" is therefore ambiguous: direct carry
of an old buffer and a trajectory changed by that buffer are different.

For the source-qualified unnormalized recurrence, updates 93…165 give

    M165 = p*M92 + innovation,       p=.9^73,
    DeltaM165 = p*DeltaM92 + DeltaInnovation.

Here Delta means warmup minus matched constant .9. Define the remainder
by subtraction of saved FP32 vectors in FP64. That is an exact algebraic
decomposition of those records; interpreting it as a weighted sum of new
clipped gradients is subject to accumulated FP32 multiply/add rounding.
The residual is not noise, an independently observed gradient, or the whole
causal effect of training after the switch.

A small direct-carry term would show that any substantial late buffer
separation is regenerated by later trajectory-dependent updates rather
than just retaining a large numerical multiple of M92. It would **not**
show the early schedule was forgotten: it already changed the weights and
other state that generate those updates. This distinction matters to a
principled optimizer story and avoids another local-loss attribution gate.

## What results would change the account

- **Small carry, persistent radius/turn ordering in the new pairs:** a
  concrete mechanical state outlives direct old-buffer carry. This supports
  an endogenous-relative-step/history account as a candidate. It neither
  isolates radii as the cause nor determines whether current progress is
  faster than the control's at matched learning stage.
- **Small carry, geometry differences largely converge or fail to transfer:**
  the persistent mechanical-rate version is weakened. The retained endpoint
  advantage may be an early head start or may live in other learned/adaptive
  state. Do not subdivide the complement automatically.
- **Appreciable or unresolved carry:** a direct buffer contribution remains
  plausible at the retained endpoint; no "history erased" conclusion.
- **Mixed PD/SOAP/seed results:** report them as mixed. One favorable method
  cannot become a general momentum principle.

No fresh material geometry threshold should be chosen to reproduce the
known SOAP25 numbers. Present its magnitude and the new pairs' signed
ordering/relaxation honestly. If a formal geometry pass/fail condition is
wanted, declare it before reading the new pairs and disclose the prior
exposure. Even a positive structural result is not a norm-control remedy.

## Two distinct alternatives, and why they rank lower now

**Moving input-metric orientation.** The older paired marginal panel retains
full attention-input C at several training stages. Unlike the closed mean-
route/scalar-pooling story, one could ask whether covariance orientation
changes even when its spectrum is similar, exposing a moving metric that
momentum must track. But it is mainly the older 1M family and the time gaps
are much longer than a momentum window. New rank0 sidecars are only proxies
for online owner caches. This cannot presently identify the matched 16M
warmup mechanism, so it is lower priority than the qualified history contrast.

**Context-dependent predictive gain.** Existing per-token endpoint scores
could ask whether schedule gains concentrate where longer causal context
is available, rather than in first-token/prior prediction. That is a genuine
architecture question distinct from temperature fitting or a parameter
factorial. However, the six endpoints are not matched for learning progress,
first-position support is small, and the prior frequency-profile analysis
already demonstrated this phase-confounding trap. Without a specific
progress-control premise, another positional binning would risk a feature
hunt; do not open it merely because arrays are available.

The chosen buffer/geometry check has limited scope but better identification
and lower cost. Its value is to distinguish **raw state carry from regenerated state**, not
to locate every functional memory channel or manufacture an optimizer proposal. Completed main intervention evidence
should be integrated when the comparison is complete, rather than deriving
a beta effect from the presently isolated PD-top .8 result or using these
observer diagnostics to manage the main runs.
