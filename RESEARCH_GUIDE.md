# Research guide

The current study describes Muon momentum and applied-update spectra and
compares them with AdamW adaptive updates in a fixed-width depth study. A
concentrated spectrum is not, by itself, an optimization limitation. Ordinary
next-token validation NLL remains the training outcome; geometry supports
mechanistic questions without substituting for loss progress.

Before a substantial new branch, record the observable limitation (or missing
premise), proposed mechanism, simplest competing explanation, comparison that
would distinguish them, outcomes that change the next decision, and bounded
resource cost. Keep this argument in the existing study protocol. Seek a fresh
independent peer discussion before a major scientific commitment or a growing
repair cascade. Routine engineering and execution checks need no ceremony.

Match the claim to the whole learning process: architecture, initialization,
optimizer state, precision, schedule, data exposure and batch all matter.
Fixed tokens are not fixed compute or fixed tokens per parameter. Normalized
spectra remove magnitude; retain norms. Distinguish pre-NS momentum from the
actual post-NS direction, and both from gradients and parameter-write rounding.

Keep baseline and instruments qualified. Diagnose numerical failures before
interpreting them scientifically. Keep original criteria and failed attempts;
never silently rename an abandoned variant or expand a tuning grid. A single
seed or matched recipe does not establish isolated causality or a scaling law.

Preserve frozen execution sources and historical metadata. Do not rewrite
absolute paths embedded in old checkpoints: compatibility links currently
resolve them. The shared Python environment and FineWeb source are documented
in README.md. Verify queued/running processes before moving or restarting work.

Keep RESEARCH_STATE.md as the short current map and raw evidence in logs/.
Historical recommendations and attached conversations are not permission to
launch jobs. Current user steering governs. No extra approval stage is added.
